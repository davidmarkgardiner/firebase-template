# Story 2.1: Product Listing and Category Pages

## Status
Draft

## Story
**As a** customer browsing for Honduras specialty coffee,
**I want** organized product listing pages with categories and sorting options,
**so that** I can efficiently discover coffees that match my preferences.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. Product listing page displays all available coffees in grid format
2. Category-specific pages for Estate Reserve, Mountain Select, and Seasonal
3. Sort functionality (price, name, popularity, newest)
4. Pagination for large product sets
5. Product count display and results summary
6. Featured products section on main listing page
7. "New arrivals" and "Best sellers" sections
8. Filter persistence in URL for bookmarkable results
9. Loading states with skeleton components
10. Empty states for no results or out of stock
11. SEO optimization with meta tags and structured data

```
/products - Main product listing
/products?category=estate-reserve - Estate Reserve
/products?category=mountain-select - Mountain Select
```
